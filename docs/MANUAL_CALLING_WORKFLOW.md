# Manual calling workflow

Application prepares → Human confirms → Device opens `tel:` link → Human speaks → Human records result → Application creates next action.

The system does not control a SIM, place calls automatically, record, listen to, or transcribe calls, bypass device restrictions, or determine call status. The user's normal mobile plan applies. On macOS, a configured calling app may handle `tel:` links; Roshan can instead dial the displayed number on his phone.

Opening a `tel:` URI does not guarantee that a call was placed or completed. Roshan records the outcome manually. A do-not-contact outcome blocks later preparation, and qualifying outcomes generate follow-up tasks automatically. No call audio is captured or processed.

## Responsible use

- Respect do-not-contact requests.
- Do not repeatedly contact uninterested businesses.
- Do not misrepresent identity or capabilities.
- Do not promise price, delivery date, or functionality without human approval.
- Maintain accurate notes.
- Comply with applicable calling and privacy rules before broader use.
